# Bank logos

The bank symbols in this folder identify the user's bank in the account lists. Each one is cropped to its symbol from
a file on Wikimedia Commons that Commons lists as public domain (below the threshold of originality). They remain
trademarks of their owners; they are used only to identify the bank, and no endorsement is implied.
